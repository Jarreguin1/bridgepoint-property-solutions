Frequently asked questions

Clear answers before you decide anything.

Every property and owner situation is different. These answers are general information, not legal, tax, financial, or real estate advice.

How does the process work?

You tell us about the property, we review the information and discuss possible next steps, and you decide whether any proposed solution works for you.

Am I required to accept an offer?

No. You are not required to accept any proposal, and submitting the form does not create an obligation to sell.

Do you purchase properties that need repairs?

We consider properties in many conditions, including properties needing repairs. Each situation is reviewed individually.

Do I need to clean the property first?

Usually, the current condition can be discussed as part of the review. Please share what you know in the inquiry form.

Are there commissions?

Bridgepoint Property Solutions is not a real estate brokerage and does not charge a brokerage commission. Transaction terms vary, so review any proposed agreement carefully.

What kinds of properties do you consider?

We may consider single-family homes, multifamily properties, condos, townhomes, land, rentals, vacant properties, and other real estate depending on the market and situation.

How quickly can a transaction close?

Timing depends on the property, title, parties involved, documentation, and other circumstances. No closing date is guaranteed.

What happens after I submit the form?

We review the property details and may contact you to ask follow-up questions or discuss possible next steps.

Are you a real estate brokerage?

No. Bridgepoint Property Solutions is a real estate investment business and is not a real estate brokerage.

What if I am facing foreclosure?

Foreclosure situations can be time-sensitive and legally complex. You may wish to consult qualified legal, tax, financial, housing, or real estate professionals about your individual circumstances.
